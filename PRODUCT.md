# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

La French Tech Oslo serves founders, operators, investors, students, partners, and tech talent connected to the French startup ecosystem in Oslo and Norway. Visitors are likely discovering the local chapter, checking whether the community is legitimate, or looking for a practical way to follow updates and join.

## Product Purpose

This site introduces Oslo's La French Tech chapter, explains what the community does, and converts interested visitors into followers or free members. Success means the page feels credible enough for institutional partners while still energetic enough for startup people, and makes the next step obvious.

## Brand Personality

Official, entrepreneurial, connected. The tone should respect La French Tech's established brand while feeling active, local, and useful rather than bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Do not make it too playful, informal, or mascot-like. Do not dilute the existing La French Tech identity. Avoid generic startup landing-page tropes, interchangeable SaaS card grids, gratuitous gradients, and visuals that feel unrelated to Oslo or the French Tech network.

## Design Principles

- Lead with legitimacy: make the La French Tech identity and Oslo chapter clear immediately.
- Bridge official and startup energy: credible enough for institutions, direct enough for founders.
- Keep the community offer practical: events, introductions, updates, and the private WhatsApp group should feel concrete.
- Preserve the brand: extend La French Tech rather than inventing a separate local identity.
- Make joining low-friction: following and becoming a free member should remain the primary actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for future styled surfaces. Keep text readable, preserve keyboard access for calls to action, do not rely on color alone, and respect reduced-motion preferences when motion is introduced.
